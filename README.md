# 🍳 Chow

An iOS app I built to save recipes and turn them into a weekly grocery list.

<!-- Add 2–3 screenshots here: drag images into the GitHub editor and it creates the links -->

## Why I built it
I was saving recipes in too many places and losing track of them. On top of that, turning
a recipe into a meal plan that fit my serving sizes, and then working out how much of each
ingredient to buy, was completely manual. I built Chow to keep my go-to recipes in one place,
scale them up or down, and walk out of the grocery store with the right amounts of everything.

## What it does
- **Save recipes:** keep my most-used recipes in one place
- **Scale servings:** adjust any recipe up or down and the ingredient amounts update
- **Plan the week:** pick meals for the week ahead
- **Generate a grocery list:** combine ingredients across the week's meals into one list
  with the right quantities, so nothing gets forgotten

## How I built it
I built Chow with [Rork](https://rork.com), an AI app builder. I iterated on prompts to design
the features and UI, reviewed and edited the Swift code, and tested on my own phone.
It runs on my iPhone and isn't on the App Store.

**Stack:** Swift · SwiftUI · Rork

## What I learned
**Design matters more than I expected.** Most of my iteration went into making the app look
and feel like something I'd actually be excited to open. I tried building it on other platforms
first, but native SwiftUI made a huge difference in how smooth it felt, which is why I
ultimately went with Rork.
